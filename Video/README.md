## 🎯 Key Topics Covered Across the Videos
1. **The False Mastery Problem**: Why traditional percentage scores fail to distinguish genuine mastery from surface guessing and elimination tricks.
2. **DeepCognitiveNet Architecture**: Multi-branch neural network combining 2-layer Bi-GRU, Self-Attention Pooling, and tabular psychometric features.
3. **Two-Tier Cognitive Probing**: Verifying underlying scientific and causal principles to prevent lucky guessing.
4. **Universal Curriculum Generation**: Live factual research synthesis across any academic subject without template jargon.
5. **Interactive Remedial Interventions**: Vis.js knowledge gap maps and targeted 3-question remedial drills.
